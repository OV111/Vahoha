## Plan

| Stage | Users | How | Exit test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Talk | 0-15 | 5 DMs a day, about 1 in 4 reply. 15-minute interviews (`user-interviews.md`), then watch them take the Layer 1 test, then the $15 ask | 3 of 15 pay (`validate-idea.md`) |
| 2. Hand-hold | 15-40 | Every interviewee, plus referrals asked in each call. Onboard personally, read the funnel weekly | Week-1 return and second layer started (`metrics.md`) |
| 3. Earn attention | 40-100 | Show real work in public, answer questions where the persona hangs out, ship a public proof page | Signups that arrive without a DM |
