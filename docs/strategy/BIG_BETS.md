# Big bets: the road to a $100M company

> 2026-10-18 brainstorm. Ideas, not commitments. None is tested with users.

## Thesis

AI makes writing code cheap. Understanding becomes the scarce thing. Vahoha
teaches deep understanding and proves it in a way that is hard to fake
(voice-verified, depth-measured).

## The math

A $100M valuation needs roughly $10M a year in revenue. At $20 a month that is
about 42,000 paying learners, and learners cancel once they are hired. Selling
only to learners rarely gets there. Businesses paying gets there.

## Ideas, small to big

| # | Idea | Who pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Verified understanding credential: learning is free, the voice-verified depth score is paid | Learners |
| 2 | "Go deeper" pages as the best deep explanations online, a free traffic engine (SEO and AI answers) | Nobody directly; fixes distribution |
| 3 | Hiring: defended evidence replaces take-home tests, which AI now solves | Employers, per hire or search |
| 4 | Cheat-proof oral grading for courses (OralExam.ai already sells here) | Universities, bootcamps |
| 5 | Team upskilling: "do your juniors understand the AI code they ship?" | Companies, team plans, low churn |
| 6 | Explain your PR: a GitHub app where the author of an AI-assisted PR explains it by voice before merge. Moves Vahoha into developer tooling | Engineering orgs |
| 7 | Reverse the roadmap: learn from your own job's codebase | Companies or learners |

## Recommended path

- **Wedge:** 1 + 2. Learners, deep content, voice verification.
- **Expansion:** 5 + 6. Companies, where the $100M is. Same engine (voice,
  rubric, quote-verified grading), new buyer.

## Riskiest assumption

Employers trust and care about voice-verified understanding. If they do not,
ideas 1, 3, 5 and 6 weaken.

Cheapest test ($0, one week): show one evidence page to 5 hiring managers or
senior developers. Pass: 3 say it would change whether they interview the
person. Same as experiment 3 in `docs/founder/validate-idea.md`.

## Open question
